    La Maison

    Silk first, scent after

    Soie Noire began in an attic in Lyon, among bolts of black silk that a family of weavers never sold. The perfumer kept one rule from them: make less, and make it well.

    When a batch is gone it stays gone until the next one is released. Nothing is reformulated to meet demand, and nothing is made in a hurry.
